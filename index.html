Research capture tool only. It records and summarises movement; it does not judge good vs. bad movement or make any clinical claim. Shoulder position and elbow angle are geometric estimates from head and wrist pose (Quest does not track the shoulder or elbow), so validate against control data before relying on them. Each attempt also fires a mq:attempt event on document with the samples and metrics, ready for a model to listen to.
